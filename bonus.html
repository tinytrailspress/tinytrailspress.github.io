What's in each pack

Printing tips

  1. Print on plain letter-size paper (8.5 × 11 in).
  2. Choose “Actual size” or 100% in your printer settings, not “Fit to page.”
  3. For the tangram pieces, thicker paper or card stock works best.
  4. Print only the pages you need. Each pack is a single PDF.
